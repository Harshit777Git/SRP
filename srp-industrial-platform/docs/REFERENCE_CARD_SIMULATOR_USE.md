# Reference cards in the simulator

The simulator's **Reference Cards** page reads the two complete numerical surface traces in `state/vendor/echometer-session20.zip` directly. It preserves sample order, converts inches to metres and lbf to newtons, and plots the latest synthetic surface stroke as a dashed overlay. The ZIP remains the source; no field record is copied into training. The Session 9 bundle contains a native TAM container, but no separately verified ASCII trace for this viewer.

The first card has 132 points and 1.843 m measured travel; the second has 216 points and 1.153 m measured travel. These measured values come from `data/evaluation/real_acquisition_audit.json`. Their filenames are **not fault ground truth**. Their timebase, equipment configuration, physical well identity and intended units beyond the vendor DYN convention have not been independently confirmed. The overlay is therefore a geometry and load-scale inspection, not a fault-mechanism validation.

The physics simulator uses a four-bar surface motion, a discretized elastic rod, chamber pressure, intake/discharge valve conductances and measured engineering units. Those concepts align with the card-based studies listed in `docs/REAL_DATASET_ACQUISITION.md`. Exact distribution matching cannot be demonstrated: the three papers did not yield raw card exports, the public controller CSVs lack verified fault labels and usage rights, and the vendor examples lack equipment and operating context. Current synthetic runs remain synthetic-only. In particular, valve leakage and pump wear share a conductance effect, and incomplete fillage and restricted inflow share delayed compression behavior; see `docs/FAULT_SEPARABILITY_ANALYSIS.md`.

Before claiming field-matched synthetic faults, obtain permission-cleared records with full numerical traces, units, stroke timing, well geometry, and independent fault confirmation. Match healthy load and stroke distributions per well, then compare fault features on held-out wells. Do not tune a fault mechanism to a filename or an unlabeled curve.

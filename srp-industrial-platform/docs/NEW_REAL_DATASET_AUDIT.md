The NK dataset card describes hourly measured parameters, **not full dynacards**. Its asserted measurement origin is plausible but was not independently validated against the original Excel/SCADA system. Its Apache-2.0 declaration and explicit owner obligations must both be respected. It can inform ranges for SPM, pump fillage and load extrema, but these are not enough to verify fault shapes or individual stroke geometry.

## Synthetic-model diagnosis and concrete next steps

The historical synthetic archive has **800 runs, 100 per class** and a grouped 480/160/160 train/calibration/test split. The old LightGBM test macro F1 was **0.570**; its train/calibration/test macro F1 was **0.869/0.637/0.570**, indicating a large unseen-configuration gap. The seed-13 confusion matrix (`data/evaluation/v2_full_ml.json`) contains 10 standing-valve leaks called healthy, eight traveling-valve leaks called pump wear, eight restricted-inflow cases called incomplete fillage and five incomplete-fillage cases called healthy. Balance is not the cause. Archived severities cover 28–96%, not low severity or onset transitions.

Physical collision is demonstrated: 75% traveling-valve leak and 50% pump wear enter the same effective outflow conductance and produced exactly identical surface and reconstructed downhole traces in a controlled comparison. Restricted inflow and incomplete fillage both produce delayed compression; low standing-valve leakage can resemble healthy. No classifier can reliably separate identical cards without extra evidence. Add measured intake-pressure response, valve test or service confirmation before claiming those distinct causes. Consider reporting a shared hydraulic symptom class when only cards are available.

The CNN uses fixed 3 m/30 kN scaling, retaining absolute amplitude but leaving well geometry as a nuisance variable. Per-well physical normalization plus absolute-scale features should be tested on held-out configurations. Synthetic surface and inverse downhole traces share a 200-point grid and are correlated evidence, not independent measurements. Existing inverse/forward check (`data/evaluation/wave.json`) reports 128 N load RMSE and 2.31% of load range for one clean healthy SRP_001 case; this is **synthetic** reconstruction evidence, not field validation. Enforce aligned time arrays and consistent phase rotation in training and live inference, as the remediation path now does.

The later experimental physics-feature baseline reached 0.667 historical macro F1 but only **0.498 on 96 fresh simulated runs**; its dual-card CNN reached 0.407 on that fresh set (`docs/REAL_VS_SYNTHETIC_EVALUATION.md`). These results do not meet production promotion criteria. Freeze production. Next evaluate normalized features, mechanism-identifiable label groups, low-severity coverage and wave-equation sensitivity on genuinely unseen simulation configurations. Use NK hourly trends only as an external range check. Obtain rights and source metadata for the 76 controller cards before retaining them for plotting. Seek independently adjudicated field cards across distinct wells for any supervised real-data training.

## Additional local source — TWM tutorial bundle and installed Recall example

The exact user-supplied `OneDrive_2026-11-16 (2).zip` was safely audited and its nested 125-file TWM tutorial bundle was extracted, SHA-256 checked, and retained under `data/external_reference/onedrive_twm_examples/`. The installed TWM was opened through Recall Mode and the bundled `Examples > Gas Interference` case was successfully recalled. TWM displayed one annotated case, a high-fluid-level acoustic shot, one DYNA record and two valve records (1998; serial HLT023). The UI exposed measured and calculated valve/load/pressure values. The tutorial comments state 40% fillage and describe gas interference, but neither those annotations nor filenames establish independently verified fault labels. The TWM Data Export wizard was completed for the Examples group and produced 45 calculated summary rows in `data/external_reference/twm_export_20261117.csv`. It did not expose raw ordered load–position samples, so this adds **zero full numerical cards and zero labeled training records**. The export has missing markers and suspect unit/scale values (including SPM reported as 4,444–30,202); values are retained unchanged and excluded from training. See `reports/PHASE3_2/TWM_RECALL_INSPECTION.md`.

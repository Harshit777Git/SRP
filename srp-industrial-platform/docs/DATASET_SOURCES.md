# Dataset sources

The [machine-readable manifest](../data/dataset_manifest.json) records checksums, paths, provenance, units and record counts for downloaded or generated files.

| Source | Material reviewed | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| [SRP component simulation](https://github.com/rmzmaher/srp-component-oriented-simulation) | Two lumped Kelvin–Voigt sections, symbolic equation assembly and Euler stepping, sinusoidal surface excitation and smoothed valve load | Reference review. The simulator in this workspace uses multiple elements per rod section and pressure-driven valve flow. No reuse license identified. |
| [PLOS ONE Li et al. (2025), S1 Data](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0326973) | Eight XLSX files: DC1–DC4 contain 4 surface cards × 144 ordered displacement/load pairs; ED1–ED4 contain 41,699 rows with three-phase voltage/current, power, power-factor and remarks fields. Eight lossless CSV derivatives and per-column profiles are generated by `scripts/audit_plos_supplement.py`. | CC BY 4.0 with attribution. Paper describes both measured electrical data and electrical series generated from cards, but does not map origin per ED workbook. No timestamps, well IDs, fault labels, units, remarks-code definitions, or DC/ED crosswalk. Keep as engineering reference only; no supervised fault training. See [audit](../reports/PHASE3_2/PLOS_SUPPLEMENT_AUDIT.md) and [provenance](../data/external_reference/plos_0326973_s001/PROVENANCE.json). |
| [Public controller exports](https://github.com/mulzahrian/production_optimization/tree/main/csv_file) | Two pipe-delimited CSVs downloaded for restricted local audit: 76 cards (one well), 10,119 ordered surface pairs at 132–134 points/card. The September file has blank downhole arrays; the October file's 43 downhole position/load arrays are identical to each other, not usable paired downhole data. Units and equipment metadata remain unverified; no fault labels. | `RESTRICTED`: no data license found and the source maintainer says all rights reserved. Local plotting/import-format checks only; no redistribution or training. See [acquisition audit](../reports/PHASE3_2/REAL_DATA_AND_REFERENCE_UPDATE.md) and [provenance](../data/external_reference/production_optimization/PROVENANCE.json). |
| [chuymtz/dynacard](https://github.com/chuymtz/dynacard) | `Everitt/case1s.mat`: one 456-point numeric X/F surface-card input; `PUnit.mat`: 360-point kinematic vector; Everitt–Jennings reconstruction script. | One engineering reference input only; its measured/simulated/digitized origin and units are not established. No downhole trace, well metadata or fault labels. No repository data license found. | `PERMISSION_UNVERIFIED`; retain for internal engineering inspection only. Not eligible for training or redistribution. See `data/external_reference/chuymtz_dynacard_source/PROVENANCE.json`. |
| [Chang et al., Sensors 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7582724/) | Paper reports 8,000 field-measured cards, 1,000 for each of eight classes, normalized to 200 point pairs. No numeric archive was found in the article or supplements reviewed. | Promising author-access lead only; paper CC BY does not establish a separate license for the underlying records. It reports random 80/20 card split; evaluate well/run leakage if access is granted. | Not downloaded; zero locally available records; request numerical traces, labels, well IDs and dataset-use terms from authors. |
| [QRod](https://www.echometer.com/software/qrod) | Predictive wave-equation program. Inputs include stroke rate, stroke length, pump diameter, depth and tubing anchor. Outputs include polished-rod loads, torque and pump capacity. | Documentation reviewed. |
| [libzrod](https://pypi.org/project/libzrod/) | Predictive and diagnostic solvers with Gibbs / Everitt–Jennings capabilities and authenticated access | Optional comparative reference. |
| [Eisner, Langbauer, Fruhwirth, 2021](https://pure.unileoben.ac.at/en/publications/sucker-rod-pump-downhole-dynamometer-card-determination-based-on-/) | Open-access engineering paper on finite element downhole-card reconstruction. Stored in original PDF format in `data/external_reference`. | Creative Commons Attribution. The PDF provides published comparisons and methodology. It supplies zero raw telemetry records to this workspace. |
| [Sandia downhole dynamometer database report](https://digital.library.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metadc677984/) | Describes direct pressure, temperature, load and acceleration measurements and processing at multiple rod depths | Literature reference. Numerical instrument files unavailable in the reviewed archive. |
| [Changqing operating-condition study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/earth-science/articles/10.3389/feart.2022.852633/full) | Describes labeled field cards | Literature reference. No permission-cleared, downloadable synchronized field telemetry verified for use. |

Synthetic rows and offline truth are in `data/generated`. The training archive contains grouped synthetic cards and per-run equipment configurations. The SQLite/PostgreSQL stores capture samples through MQTT during live runs.

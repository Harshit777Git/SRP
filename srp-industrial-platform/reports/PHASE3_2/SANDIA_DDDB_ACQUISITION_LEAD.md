# Sandia Downhole Dynamometer Data Base

Verified source: [Sandia announcement dated 20 October 1997](https://newsreleases.sandia.gov/new-cd-rom-available-free-from-sandia-national-laboratories-reveals-nitty-gritty-details-of-downhole-oil-well-environment/), reviewed 2026-11-17.

Sandia describes field tests at six producing US wells during 1996, using five downhole instruments per test to collect load-related strain, acceleration, pressure and temperature. The original distribution was a CD-ROM containing over 60 MB. This is a promising independent reconstruction benchmark candidate, not an acquired dataset.

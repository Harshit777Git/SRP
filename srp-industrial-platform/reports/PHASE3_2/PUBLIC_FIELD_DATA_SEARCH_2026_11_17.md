The article reports 1,650 measured condition samples from 60 pumping units over three years and 11 conditions; the described dynamometer and electrical cards are binary images, not necessarily raw ordered vectors. ## Newly verified higher-priority lead: Mossoró field-card study

Nascimento et al. report more than 50,000 measured dynamometer cards from 38 sucker-rod pumping wells in Mossoró, Brazil. They describe expert classification across eight operating modes plus two sensor-fault classes. Their workflow appears to use normalized 100-point downhole-card representations and the paper explicitly says the data are available on request from the corresponding author, André Maitelli (`maitelli@dca.ufrn.br`). The exact downloadable files, per-class counts, source-well identifiers, fault evidence, split protocol, surface-card availability, and data reuse permission must be confirmed with the authors. Published results (including high card-level accuracies) are not independent validation of this SRP simulator and must not be treated as expected performance. No files have been received and no request has been sent. A request draft is included in `docs/REAL_DATA_AUTHOR_REQUEST_DRAFT.md`.

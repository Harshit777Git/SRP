# SRP ML readiness

**Production multiclass fault diagnosis remains unvalidated.** The archive has 800 synthetic runs, 100 per class. Its LightGBM grouped historical test macro F1 is 0.570; the later physics-feature candidate reaches 0.667 on that historical comparison but only 0.498 on 96 fresh synthetic runs. Neither result is field accuracy. Zero independently verified real fault labels were acquired. No classifier was retrained or promoted in this audit.

The eight synthetic labels are available for controlled experiments only. The old confusion matrix shows standing-valve leak vs healthy, traveling-valve leak vs pump wear, and restricted inflow vs incomplete fillage. The physics itself can make different causes identical: 75% traveling-valve leakage and 50% pump wear produced the same two card traces in a controlled comparison. The inverse downhole card is calculated from the surface card and cannot resolve information absent at the surface. Fixed channel scales retain amplitude but transfer poorly across equipment, while severity training omitted 0.1–28% and onset periods.

Ready for **research experiments**: unsupervised anomaly exploration and provider-fillage estimation on NK hourly SRP parameters, with complete-well and future-time holdouts and no operational deployment. A ridge baseline using SPM, rod-weight extrema and dynamometer area had 27,874 complete, quality-filtered rows across seven wells. Leave-one-whole-well-out mean MAE was **25.80 percentage points**, versus **32.88** for a training-mean baseline. Every held-out well had negative R². The provider fillage target is an estimate, not independently measured truth, and the area-to-fillage calculation lineage is unknown. This is **not deployable fillage accuracy**; details are in `reports/SRP_NK_FILLAGE_BASELINE.json`. The card classification, valve, gas, rod and fluid-pound tasks require adjudicated labels and time-resolved cards. Motor, pump efficiency and production models require measured electrical, flow or production targets. RUL needs event dates and censoring. Detailed prerequisites and status are in `reports/SRP_MODEL_FEASIBILITY_MATRIX.xlsx`.

Next experiment: freeze the existing synthetic archive, evaluate physically normalized and absolute-scale features side by side on held-out configurations, test merged symptom classes for physically indistinguishable causes, and measure reconstruction sensitivity to geometry/damping. Then obtain independent field wells and service-confirmed labels before any production promotion. The NK dataset supports range checks; its `dynamometer_area` has negative values and its source year was assigned, so quality review is mandatory.
